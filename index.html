Frequently asked

Everything you need to know before joining.

Do I need to pay before I can start a conversation?

Yes. Members must pay their one-time fee and complete approval before messaging can begin.

How do I pay?

Members will pay using Lipa na M-Pesa with the till number added later by the admin.

Can I browse profiles before paying?

Yes, after completing your profile. Messaging remains locked until payment and verification are approved.

How does safety work?

All accounts are adults only, and members can block, report, and verify profiles in a controlled environment.
