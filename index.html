Frequently asked

Everything you need to know before joining.

How do I pay?

Choose Lipa na M-Pesa, then Buy Goods and Services. Enter the TamuDating Till number 1875493, confirm the amount, and complete payment on your phone. Then submit the M-Pesa transaction code for manual review.

What happens after I pay?

Submit the M-Pesa transaction code. An admin will check the payment manually. Messaging is unlocked only after approval for both people.

Can I browse before paying?

Yes, once your profile is complete. A payment and admin approval are required before you can message.

Is this prototype accepting payments?

No. The payment flow is a manual-site preview and still needs full review before live use. Do not submit real payment information until the full workflow is validated.
